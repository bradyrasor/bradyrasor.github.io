Quant research · Ongoing

Options Trading Team — an AI agent desk

A team of specialized AI agents modeled on a real trading desk: a scout, technical analyst, strategist, risk manager with veto power, portfolio manager, execution trader, position monitor and journal. It runs in the cloud every trading hour through Robinhood's official agentic trading interface, in paper mode, with risk limits and a kill switch enforced in code.

  • Backtested classic intraday, oversold-bounce and post-earnings setups — and documented that they lose money once real spreads and time decay are counted.
  • Recorded real option quotes each round to replace modeled prices, finding real spreads averaged ~10% vs. the 6% the model assumed.
  • Found the first rule to hold up on unseen data: a 20-day breakout filter on large caps in a calm market regime.
  • Built a live dashboard comparing each strategy's P&L to the S&P 500 and a go-live checklist no strategy can skip.

Council of Agents — an AI review board

A review board of AI agents that challenges everything I build before it ships. Six standing roles (chair, strategist, skeptic, researcher, builder, advocate) plus specialists for trading and design. Each agent writes its position blind, then challenges its peers; the chair rules, and every change of mind is saved as a lesson in that agent's own notes. Rounds, dissent and each agent's track record show on a live dashboard tab.

Built November 2026. Outcomes are still being graded; the first review is scheduled for December 16.
